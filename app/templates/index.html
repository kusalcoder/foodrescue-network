How it works

1. Providers list surplus food

Restaurants, grocers, and caterers post what's available, how much, and when it can be picked up.

2. Recipients request it

Verified shelters and community organizations browse nearby listings and request what they need.

3. Food gets picked up

Providers accept requests, schedule a pickup window, and confirm once the handoff is complete.
