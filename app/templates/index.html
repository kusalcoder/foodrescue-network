How it works

1. Providers list food donations

Restaurants, bakeries, grocers, and caterers post available food donations with quantity, pickup window, and location.

2. Orphanages & Receivers request food

Verified orphanages, shelters, and relief organizations search nearby donations and providers to request what they need.

3. Confirmation & Direct Contact

Providers accept requests, schedule pickup times, and both sides get mutual contact details (phone, email) and location directions.
